Production process

From first enquiry to finished order

A clear sequence keeps every order predictable — from the brief to the packed garments.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You send your requirements through the quote form or contact us directly.

  2. 02

    Requirements

    We clarify tasks, working conditions, quantities and sizes.

  3. 03

    Model and fabric

    Models, fabrics, colours and logo placement are selected.

  4. 04

    Sample

    A sample is prepared so the garment can be assessed before production.

  5. 05

    Approval

    You confirm the sample, size range and order details.

  6. 06

    Cutting

    Fabric is marked and cut according to the approved patterns.

  7. 07

    Sewing and branding

    Garments are sewn and finished with embroidery or print where required.

  8. 08

    Quality check and handover

    Finished items are inspected, packed and handed over.
